About Me

Hi, I'm Avi! I'm a student at San Diego State University studying Management Information Systems with a minor in Computer Science.

Outside of school, I enjoy hiking and climbing. I'm originally from Portland Oregon, so the past mountains I've done were snowy peaks but now in San Diego, I've had to learn new scrambling techniques.

Why I like the Sierra Nevada

The Sierra Nevada has amazing lakes, rocky peaks, and mountain views. Mount Agassiz, Cloudripper, and Mount Langley are three hikes I wanted to share on this website.

Future goals

I want to keep building my climbing skills and explore more mountains. Some of my goals in the next couple years is to climb North Palisade via the glacier, Shasta, Whitney, and Rainer.
